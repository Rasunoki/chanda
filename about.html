Where "sauce" meets "wrap"

SauSaWrap was created to fill the demand for practical and appetizing on-the-go meals, standing out by uniquely combining Filipino flavors within a burrito or shawarma-style wrap. Each wrap features a fresh egg wrapper, encasing a choice of savory Filipino dishes with either plain or garlic rice, specifically Creamy Chicken Adobo, Mushroom Sisig, or Tapa Talong. Customers can further personalize their meal with a variety of dipping sauces, such as creamy béchamel, sweet sauce, or chili garlic mayo, and complete their meal with refreshing iced tea or soft drinks. This ensures a delicious, flavorful, and accessible dining experience at an ideal serving size.

Mission

Our passion is to serve quality food products, quick service, and a fun-dining experience that brings satisfaction to the faces of Valenzuelanos every season of the year.

Vision

We foresee being one of the leading food companies in our community, providing local opportunities and a healthier lifestyle that contributes to better and more sustainable operations.

Business Goals

To meet the increasing demand for convenient, affordable, high-quality portable meals — primarily for busy young professionals and students — with a customizable meal-to-go solution at an accessible price point, securing a unique position in the competitive food industry.

Sauce+Wrap

Story Behind the Name

"SauSa" highlights the flavorful sauces that enhance every meal, while "Wrap" reflects the convenient burrito or shawarma-style presentation. SauSaWrap transforms familiar Filipino comfort food into a portable meal — easy to eat anytime, anywhere.

It's street food innovation: delicious, affordable, and suited to busy lifestyles, blending taste, convenience, and Filipino culture in every bite.

The Company — ElevAte

ElevAte is the company behind SauSaWrap, built to elevate the dining experience with creative, flavorful, and affordable food. It values innovation, customer satisfaction, teamwork, and sustainability — and aims to become a trusted local brand, especially among students and young professionals.
