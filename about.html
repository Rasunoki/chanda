Where "sauce" meets "wrap"

SauSaWrap was created to meet the rising demand for practical, appetizing, and customizable on-the-go meals. Our concept brings together beloved Filipino flavors, wrapped in a fresh egg wrapper and paired with plain or garlic rice, to create a filling and satisfying meal. Each SauSaWrap is made to be convenient without sacrificing taste, giving busy customers a delicious option they can enjoy anytime, anywhere.

Mission

Our passion is to serve quality food products, quick service, and a fun-dining experience that brings satisfaction to the faces of Valenzuelanos every season of the year.

Vision

We foresee being one of the leading food companies in our community, providing local opportunities and a healthier lifestyle that contributes to better and more sustainable operations.

Business Goals

Our goal is to provide accessible, high-quality portable meals that fit the fast-paced lifestyles of young professionals and students. By focusing on flavor, convenience, and value, SauSaWrap aims to secure a unique and profitable position in the competitive food industry while continuously improving our offerings and customer experience.

Sauce+Wrap

Story Behind the Name

The name 'Sau' refers to our signature sauces, while 'Sa' stands for 'sarap'—the Filipino word for delicious. Together, they represent the flavorful and tasty experience found in every bite.

'Wrap' reflects the convenient burrito or shawarma-style presentation that makes our meals easy to enjoy on the go. Together, SauSaWrap stands for a fun, flavorful, and practical way to enjoy Filipino-inspired comfort food.

The Company — ElevAte

ElevAte is the passionate parent company behind SauSaWrap, dedicated to elevating the dining experience through creative and affordable food concepts. Guided by values of teamwork, innovation, and customer satisfaction, ElevAte focuses on building brands that bring people together over good food. With SauSaWrap, ElevAte aims to offer a modern take on local flavors while creating opportunities for growth within the community.
