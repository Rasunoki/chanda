SauSaWrap is a quick-service small dining offering savory Filipino meals wrapped in fresh egg wrappers, served with signature dipping sauces.

Unlike traditional pita or tortilla wraps, we use fresh, protein-rich egg wrappers paired with classic Filipino flavors and custom dipping sauces.

Our crowd favorites are the Creamy Chicken Adobo Wrap for a classic comfort taste and the Mushroom Sisig Wrap for a crispy, savory, and spicy kick.

Yes, you can pair your wrap with your preferred signature dipping sauce.

We offer Talong Tapa Wrap (₱79.00), Creamy Chicken Adobo Wrap (₱115.00), and Mushroom Sisig Wrap (₱135.00).

We offer three signature sauces at ₱19.00 each: White Béchamel, Sweet Soy Sauce, and Chili Garlic Mayo.

Yes, we offer 16oz Signature House-Blend Iced Tea for ₱25.00 and Mismo Soft Drinks (Coke, Sprite, and Royal) for ₱25.00 each.
